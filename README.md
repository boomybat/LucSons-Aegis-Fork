# LucSon's Aegis Fork
A (to be worked on) fork of AEGIS to further future Arma 3 pubzeusing. AEGIS is an addon made by Kelly/LH Martin for Expunged's Enhanced Zeus Modules, an Arma 3 vanilla Zeus enhancement script. Unfinished.

Currently just a complete clone of Aegis as of 9/22/2026. If Lockheed returns and wishes for me to take this down or make it private, I shall oblige. I'm only keeping this up as he stated Aegis was licensed under the MIT License, and he has been missing for a few months leaving Aegis incomplete.

Place down a helipad in the editor, copy-paste aegis.sqf into the init field then save.
OR
Place down a helipad into the editor, save it as a composition, open your compositions folder in your profile, go to the composition you saved, and replace the composition.sqe.

Current plans are to fix some bugs in the code, make a few extremely minor changes (e.g. turning headlamps red), and hopefully update it to the new version of Enhanced Zeus Modules.
